03 / Ideas in progress

Putting curiosity to work.

Entrepreneurship / Class conceptStarving
Student
Car Wash.Small idea. Real business questions.

Concept development · Not yet launched

What would it take to build a student-run service?

For a class project, I developed a mobile car-wash concept and explored its customers, costs, competition, and day-to-day operations.

After comparing feedback from two AI models, I shifted my thinking toward group bookings at apartment complexes and fraternity houses to reduce travel time. My next step would be testing demand before buying equipment.

What I took away from the project

A business idea has to work beyond the pitch. Looking at travel time, pricing, and repeat demand helped me see why getting several customers in one location could matter more than simply reaching more people.
